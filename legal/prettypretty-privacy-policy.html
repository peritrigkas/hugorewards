Pretty Pretty Rewards — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Pretty Pretty Rewards ("the app") is the digital loyalty card for Pretty Pretty nail salon. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how it's used.

Information you give us

When you join, we ask for:

  • Your name, so staff can recognise your card and the app can greet you.
  • A phone number or email address, used only to help find your card if you change phones. We don't send marketing messages to it.

Information the app creates

  • A loyalty code, shown as a QR code for staff to scan.
  • Your stamps and rewards: your current card, rewards waiting to be claimed, and cards you've redeemed or that have expired, including any limited-time promo cards.
  • An anonymous account on your phone, created automatically when you join so that only your phone can see your card. It has no password and isn't linked to any other account.

Bot protection

When you join, the app runs an automatic check (Cloudflare Turnstile) to stop automated sign-ups. Cloudflare looks at technical signals from your device and connection for this check only. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Information we do not collect

  • No payment information. The app doesn't take payments.
  • No access to your photos, contacts or files.
  • No location tracking.
  • The camera is used only by salon staff, to scan loyalty QR codes. It doesn't take, store or upload photos or video.

How your information is used

Only to run the loyalty programme: identifying your card, adding stamps, and giving rewards when you visit. We don't sell your information or share it for advertising or marketing.

Who can see your information

Your name, contact details, stamps and rewards are visible to authorised Pretty Pretty staff, who use them to run the loyalty programme in the salon. Other customers can't see them.

Where your information is stored

Your information is stored with Supabase, a database hosting provider that processes it on our behalf and doesn't use it for its own purposes. See supabase.com/privacy. It is sent over encrypted connections.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while your loyalty account exists. You can delete it at any time in the app (Account → "Delete my account and data"), or ask us to. See how to delete your account and data.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above.
