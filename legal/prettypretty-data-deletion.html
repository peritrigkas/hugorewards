Delete your Pretty Pretty Rewards account and data

Last updated: 1 November 2026

You can delete your Pretty Pretty Rewards loyalty account and all its data at any time, in either of two ways.

Option 1: delete it yourself in the app (instant)

  1. Open Pretty Pretty Rewards.
  2. Tap the account icon in the top-right corner.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete my account and data".
  4. Confirm.

Option 2: ask us by email

If you no longer have the app, email [CONTACT EMAIL] with your name or loyalty code (shown in the app under your account, or on the QR code screen). We'll confirm once your data is deleted, usually within a few working days.

What gets deleted

Your name, phone number or email, loyalty code, stamps, rewards (including any waiting to be claimed) and card history. This can't be undone. The anonymous sign-in the app created on your phone holds none of this information; uninstalling the app removes it from your phone.

See also our Privacy Policy.
