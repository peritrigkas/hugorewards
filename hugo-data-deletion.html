Delete your Hugo Rewards account and data

You can request deletion of your Hugo Rewards loyalty account and all associated data at any time, in either of two ways:

Option 1 — Delete it yourself in the app (instant)

  1. Open the Hugo Rewards app
  2. Tap the account icon in the top-right corner
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete my account and data"
  4. Confirm the deletion

This permanently removes your name, contact info, and stamp history immediately.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, or prefer to request deletion this way, email us at:

admin@hugocoffee.com]

Please include your name or loyalty code (shown in the app under My Account, or on your card's QR screen) so we can locate your record. We'll confirm once your data has been deleted, typically within a few business days.

What gets deleted

Deletion permanently removes your name, contact information (phone or email), your unique loyalty code, and your stamp count. This cannot be undone.

See also our Privacy Policy for more on how your data is used.
