Hugo Rewards — Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE —14/08/2026]

Hugo Rewards ("the app") is a digital loyalty card app for a single coffee shop. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how it's used.

Information we collect

When you join Hugo Rewards, we collect:

  • Your name — so staff can recognize your card and greet you by name in the app.
  • A phone number or email address — used to look up and restore your loyalty card if you log out or switch devices. We don't currently send marketing messages, notifications, or promotional content to this contact info.

The app also automatically creates and stores:

  • A unique loyalty code — links your card to your stamp count. Shown as a QR code for staff to scan at checkout.
  • Your stamp count — how many purchases you've made toward a free reward.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect payment information. The app does not process payments.
  • We do not access your photos, contacts, or files.
  • The app's camera feature is used only to scan QR codes for staff adding a stamp — it does not take, store, or upload photos or video.
  • We do not use the app to track your precise location.

How your information is used

Your information is used solely to operate the loyalty program: identifying your card, tracking stamps, and letting shop staff add stamps or redeem rewards when you visit. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Who can see your information

Your name, contact info, and stamp count are visible to authorized staff of the shop, who use this to operate the loyalty program in person. The app does not make your information publicly visible to other customers.

Where your information is stored

Your information is stored using Supabase, a third-party database hosting provider. Supabase stores and processes data on our behalf and does not use it for its own purposes. You can read Supabase's own privacy practices at supabase.com/privacy.

Data retention and deletion

Your information is kept for as long as your loyalty account is active. If you'd like your data deleted, contact us using the details below and we'll remove your record.

Children's privacy

Hugo Rewards is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. We do not verify age at signup.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
